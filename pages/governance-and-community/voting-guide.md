## How to vote

### 1. Go to the [DAO webpage](https://app.aragon.org/dao/base-mainnet/0x9Cf8cDF78c4f726A7a180Ed949ced9D9A1075256/dashboard)

![DAO webpage](/images/dao-webpage.png)

### 2. Connect your wallet

![DAO wallet connect #1](/images/dao-wallet-connect-1.png)  
![DAO wallet connect #2](/images/dao-wallet-connect-2.png)

### 3. (Optional) Configure your profile

![DAO profile](/images/dao-profile.png)

### 4. Delegate your voting power

> If you want to vote yourself, you have to delegate your voting power to yourself. By default your voting power is delegated to no one (0x0). You have to have your voting power delegated before the proposal is created to be able to vote on it.  
> You'll only have to do this once, not for every vote.

![DAO delegate](/images/dao-delegate.png)  
![DAO delegate select](/images/dao-delegate-select.png)  
![DAO delegate sign](/images/dao-delegate-sign.png)  
![DAO delegate success](/images/dao-delegate-success.png)

### 5. Choose a proposal

![DAO proposal select](/images/dao-proposal-select.png)  
![DAO proposal view](/images/dao-proposal-view.png)

### 6. Select your vote

![DAO proposal vote](/images/dao-proposal-vote.png)  
![DAO proposal vote select](/images/dao-proposal-vote-select.png)  
![DAO proposal vote sign](/images/dao-proposal-vote-sign.png)  
![DAO proposal vote success](/images/dao-proposal-vote-success.png)

> If the proposal was created before you delegated your voting power or you do not hold any tokens, you'll see the following screen  
> ![DAO proposal ineligible](/images/dao-proposal-ineligible.png)

### 7. Watch the voting live

![DAO proposal watch](/images/dao-proposal-watch.png)  
![DAO proposal watch votes](/images/dao-proposal-watch-votes.png)
